The Great Porcelain Spire
This Prang is intricately decorated with millions of "Benjarong" porcelain fragments and ceramic pieces imported from China. Arranged in delicate floral patterns, the structure stands as a massive ceramic sculpture towering over the riverside.
The Symbol of Mount Meru
Designed according to ancient cosmology, the central Prang represents "Mount Meru," the center of the universe. It is surrounded by four directional towers and pavilions, with guardian deities supporting each level, symbolizing the different celestial realms.
The Guardians of Wat Arun
Two legendary giants, "Thosakan" (Green) and "Sahassadecha" (White), guard the entrance to the ordination hall. They are celebrated for their exquisite plasterwork and ceramic decorations, standing as iconic rivals to the giants of Wat Pho.
