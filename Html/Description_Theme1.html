The Khmer-Inspired Central Prang
Although created in the late Ayutthaya period, it was clearly influenced by Khmer art. The majestic central tower is surrounded by four directional prangs, all situated on a single unified base.
The City of Red Bricks
The contrast between the burnt-clay red bricks, green grass, and blue sky attracts photographers worldwide. During sunset, the light reflects off the bricks, transforming them into a brilliant copper-gold glow.
Galleries and Sandstone Buddha Images
The cloister walls surrounding the central Prang now only reveal their foundations and weathered sandstone Buddha images. Most of these headless statues stand as a powerful architectural testament to the "Truth of Time."
