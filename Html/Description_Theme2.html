Pure Carrara White Marble
The only building in Thailand constructed entirely from pure white marble imported from Carrara, Italy. This material gives the hall its luminous appearance and timeless European classical elegance.
The Neoclassical Domes
The grand central dome, flanked by six smaller ones, was influenced by St. Peter's Basilica in Rome. It creates a majestic and international silhouette for the Bangkok skyline.
The Dome’s Fresco Paintings
The interior features rare "Fresco" masterpieces. These artworks depict the significant royal duties of Kings Rama I through Rama VI, a unique artistic fusion rarely found in contemporary Thai architecture.
